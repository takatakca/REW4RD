## 3. Card rules (cardholders)

- A REW4RD card is a prepaid card redeemable for goods and services at the issuing merchant and its participating locations, up to its balance. It is not a credit card or a banking product.
- **No expiry, no prohibited fees (Québec)**: in accordance with Québec's Consumer Protection Act, cards sold to consumers have **no expiry date** and carry **no activation, inactivity or administration fees**. The service applies these rules by default to every Québec merchant and offers no contrary configuration for cards sold to Québec consumers. The only recognized exceptions are those provided by law (for example, cards given free of charge for promotional or donation purposes).
- **No cash redemption**: a balance cannot be refunded in cash or converted into money, except where an applicable law requires it in a specific case (for example, certain minimal balances where the law so provides).
- **Partial redemptions**: a card may pay part of a purchase; the remaining balance stays on the card, free of charge.
- **Lost or stolen card**: with proof of purchase or possession (code, receipt, link to the customer account), the merchant can block the card and reissue the balance on a new card. A card with no means of identification cannot be replaced.
- **The balance belongs to the bearer**: anyone presenting a card's full code or QR can use it; the merchant is not responsible for a redemption made by a person to whom the holder transmitted their code. Keep your code like cash.
- In the event of manifest fraud (guessed or generated codes, cards activated without payment), the merchant may block the card in question, subject to the rights of a good-faith holder.
- A card is activated only after full payment of its purchase; a cancelled or refunded purchase order results in deactivation or counter-entry of the corresponding card.
