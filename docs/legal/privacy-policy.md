## 1. Who does what

REW4RD is a gift card program that Clover merchants offer to their customers. The merchant who sells you a card is responsible for the funds and the balances: **a card's balance is an obligation of the merchant to you**, not of TAKATAK. TAKATAK provides the technology and keeps the card ledger (codes, balances, movements) on the merchant's behalf.

## 2. Information we collect

To operate the program, the application collects only:

- **Your card code and its associated QR** — the card's identifier in the ledger (a high-entropy code; never your name by default).
- **The card's balance and movement history** — issuances, reloads, redemptions (full or partial), blocks, reissues, with the date, amount and location involved.
- **The recipient's email address** — only for a digital card, so it can be delivered (code + QR). If you buy for yourself, your email may also be used to retrieve your card.
- **Your phone number** — only if you link the card to your customer account with the merchant (the same account as the Fidélité Reward program) or check your balance by phone.
- **The personalized message** the buyer attaches to a digital card, if any.

The application **does not collect** your payment card numbers: purchases and reloads are paid directly in Clover. A physical card bought with cash at the counter can exist with no personal information attached to it at all.

## 3. Why we collect this information

- To issue, activate (after confirmed payment) and deliver your card
- To keep your card's exact balance and apply your redemptions at the counter, at the kiosk and on the merchant's website
- To retrieve your card by code, QR or phone, and to block/reissue it if lost or stolen
- To prevent fraud (repeated code attempts, abnormal velocity)
- To provide the merchant with its accounting view: cards sold, amounts redeemed, and outstanding liability (the balances owed to you) — in aggregate form, except for service related to your own card

We do **not** use your information for third-party advertising, and we **never sell** your personal information.

## 4. Consent

By buying a digital card (providing a delivery email) or linking a card to your phone number, you consent to the processing described in this policy. You may withdraw your consent by contacting the merchant or us (see section 9); withdrawal cannot retroactively erase movements already recorded in the ledger, which serve as proof of your balance — but the merchant remains bound to let you redeem the balance of a valid card.

## 5. Disclosure and hosting

- Your information is processed in Clover (Fiserv) systems when the application reads the orders and payments tied to your cards, and in TAKATAK's hosting infrastructure for the card ledger.
- It may be hosted or processed in Canada or the United States depending on the infrastructure used; it remains protected by the measures in section 7 and by our providers' contractual obligations.
- A digital card's delivery email transits through the email provider used for sending.
- We do not disclose your information to any other third party, except as required by law or with your consent.

## 6. Retention

We keep a card's movements while its balance is greater than zero and for the period required by the merchant's legal and accounting obligations afterwards (bookkeeping — a usual six-year benchmark in Canada for accounting records). An exhausted card with no activity may have its identifying information (email, linked phone) destroyed or anonymized according to the merchant's configuration, with financial movements retained in anonymized form for accounting purposes.

## 7. Security

We apply proportionate safeguards: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), high-entropy card codes displayed in truncated form after issuance, ledger access restricted to authorized personnel, authenticated administrative access, and logging of movements (append-only ledger: no movement is ever modified or deleted; any correction is a counter-entry). No system is infallible; in the event of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious harm, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and the individuals concerned, in accordance with Law 25.

## 8. Your rights (Law 25 and applicable laws)

You have the right to:

- Access your information and obtain a copy of it
- Have inaccurate or incomplete information rectified
- Withdraw your consent to optional processing (for example, phone linking)
- Request deletion of your identifying information, subject to the merchant's legal retention obligations (movement ledger, invoicing, taxes) — deletion cannot extinguish the balance of a valid card
- Request your information in a structured technological format (portability), where the law provides for it
- File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec

To exercise a right, contact the merchant or our privacy officer (section 9).
